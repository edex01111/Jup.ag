## Wallet isolation

Use a dedicated wallet per strategy or risk domain. Keep the signing process separate from your web application. Restrict who can change strategy parameters and require review for key or limit changes.

## Observability

Record a structured event for every decision:

- signal timestamp and source
- intent ID and strategy version
- quote request and selected route
- risk checks and rejection reason
- transaction signature and confirmation status
- realized output and effective price

Set alerts for repeated quote failures, rising price impact, confirmation delays, balance changes, and daily loss thresholds.

## Kill switches

Provide both an operator-controlled stop and an automatic circuit breaker. The stop should prevent new submissions while allowing reconciliation of already-submitted transactions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pause submissions">Disable the executor without deleting state.</Step>
  <Step title="Reconcile">Resolve unknown transactions and update balances.</Step>
  <Step title="Review">Inspect the latest events, routes, limits, and code version.</Step>
  <Step title="Resume gradually">Restart in dry-run or low-notional mode before restoring normal limits.</Step>
</Steps>
