## Made for the moment

Jupiter brings routing, execution, and market intelligence together so you can act while markets move. Use the app as a trader, the APIs as a developer, or the bot patterns as an operator.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="For traders" icon="wallet">Swap assets, compare routes, manage positions, and keep your workflow focused on the decision—not the plumbing.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="For developers" icon="terminal">Start with a quote, graduate to signed execution, and add token intelligence as your product grows.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="For operators" icon="shield-check">Design for slippage, latency, failures, and key safety before your first automated order reaches production.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Build your first integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a quote">Request a route for an input mint, output mint, and amount.</Step>
  <Step title="Review the route">Inspect price impact, slippage, fees, and the route plan before signing.</Step>
  <Step title="Execute safely">Sign the prepared transaction and confirm it with clear retry and failure handling.</Step>
</Steps>

<Info>Jupiter APIs are non-custodial. Never share secret keys with a server you do not control, and test with small amounts before scaling a strategy.</Info>
